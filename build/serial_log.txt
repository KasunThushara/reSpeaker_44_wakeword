
=== power_check: 0xA5 hardware all-on, then silent ===
probe 0x3C: ACK
0xA5 sent. Bus is now IDLE - the meter sees the panel alone.
  measure VCC at the module pin        (expect 3.3 V)
  measure current in series with VCC   (mA range)

  ~1 mA    = charge pump never starts
  ~0 mA    = VCC not reaching the panel
[5 s] 0xA5 held, bus idle
